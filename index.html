Three steps to a sheet that registers

The whole workflow is built around the one thing that usually goes wrong: the back not matching the front.

01

Design the front

Add text, images and shapes to the front of your sheet, and position them exactly where you want on the page.

02

Flip & align the back

Switch to Back. A mirrored ghost of the front shows through, so you can match up logos, crop marks or borders precisely.

03

Print both sides

Hit print, turn on double-sided in the dialog, and the sheet comes out of the tray registered — front and back, every time.
